# Ministerial Demo Scenario Brief

This is the baseline scenario used in the live demo flow.

- Scenario name: 2035 Lifeline Protection Plan
- Country code: JM
- Horizon year: 2035
- Hazard set: hurricane, flood, sea_level
- Budget: $30.0M
- Optimization goal: people

## Selected interventions

### Reinforce hospital roofs and backup power

- Cost: $8.0M
- Why it matters: Hardens emergency departments, reduces outages, and keeps critical care online during storms.
- Benefit tags: Protects hospitals, Improves response continuity

### Build and retrofit community shelters

- Cost: $6.5M
- Why it matters: Adds resilient shelter beds in high exposure communities with backup communications.
- Benefit tags: Protects vulnerable residents, Improves emergency readiness

### Restore mangroves and living shorelines

- Cost: $5.0M
- Why it matters: Uses nature-based buffers to damp storm surge and reduce chronic coastal erosion.
- Benefit tags: Nature-based, Reduces sea-level exposure

### Upgrade drainage and pump stations

- Cost: $9.0M
- Why it matters: Reduces urban flash flooding around dense communities and industrial estates.
- Benefit tags: Fast visible wins, Cuts repeat flood damage
