# Adaptation Intervention Catalog

These interventions are the candidate actions the orchestration layer uses during scenario simulation.

## Elevate primary coastal roads by 1m

- Intervention ID: elevate-coastal-roads
- Cost: $14.5M
- Description: Keeps evacuation corridors and emergency supply routes open after storm surge events.
- Applicable tags: coastal, logistics, roadfragile
- Benefit tags: Protects evacuation time, Unblocks ambulance access
